# avatiser. services catalog — everything we can sell
*Market-grounded asset list, July 2026. Private — never deployed.*

How to read this: every asset lists **what it is → who buys it → market rate
(what traditional agencies/freelancers charge, USD) → our rate zone → how we
produce it in the stack (FLOW / studio engine / manual pass)**. Effort is
Avatiser-hours once the pipeline is warm, not client-perceived effort.

**The 2026 market in one paragraph:** traditional production has priced itself
out — agencies average ~$42k per video project, product photography runs
$25–75 per white-bg frame and $100–500+ per lifestyle frame, and a 500-SKU
brand can burn $125–250k/year on catalog photography alone. Meanwhile AI-first
studios deliver comparable output at 5–20% of that, and buyers know it: AI
creative retainers now clear €4k–21k/month, and UGC-style content is the #1
performing paid-social format with the UGC market growing ~29% CAGR. The
buyer isn't asking "AI or not AI" anymore — they're asking "can I tell?" and
"how fast?". Avatiser's realism doctrine is aimed at exactly that question.
Our wedge: **agency-grade realism at creator-economy prices, 48h turnaround.**

1. **PDP Power Pack** — $349/SKU: 8 listing images (A5)
2. **Launch Pack** — $999: PDP pack + hero film + 3 UGC ads + 10 socials

4. **The Full World** — $2,499: complete brand campaign (the FLOW template output, all 10 deliverables + film)
5. **Creative Retainer** — $1,999/mo: 15 videos + 30 statics, 2 revision rounds, 72h SLA

**Rules:**
- Anchor high with the market comparison in every proposal ("agencies: $42k avg per video project — this pack: $999").
- Never price per-hour. Price per-asset or per-outcome; our hours collapse as the pipeline warms and that margin is ours, not the client's discount.
- First deal with any brand = free 3-visual sample (already our funnel) → PDP pack → retainer. Land, prove, expand.
- 2 revision rounds included, then $49/asset — protects against infinite-tweak clients.
