# Worked Example — Pool Blue (GYK Professional)

This was built for GYK Professional (thegyk.com) in November 2026, and its first hero was rendered and reviewed. Use it to calibrate depth, length and tone. Don't copy its choices into other briefs. The full campaign, all four shots, lives in `studio/projects/pool-blue/campaign.md`.

## The brief

> GYK Professional skincare, hydration capsule: HBC Hydrating Boba Cream 50g (clear aqua jar of blue gel capsules), Hydracloud Infusion 9-Step facial kit (lavender box, just launched), Face Scrub 200g (cream jar, aqua lid). India market. Two Indian women. Labels must match the product photos.

## Hidden reasoning (not shown to the user)

**Reading the products.**
- All three packs share aqua, white and lavender, so they can share frames.
- The boba capsules are the most photogenic texture.
- It's a hydration claim with professional-salon positioning.
- The site also sells a "Vitamin C Fairness Serum". Leave it out and say why (ASCI).

**First attempt, rejected.** A stepwell, as an ingredient/origin-style idea: "a structure built to hold water". It rendered beautifully and failed as skincare:
- the product was about 2% of the frame;
- the stagnant green water read as unhygienic;
- a warm film grade fought the aqua packaging;
- full-length framing hid the skin.

It failed the "sells" test.

**The idea that held.** A sensorial element (water) and a packaging palette that the set can own: "the jar's own aqua becomes the whole world". The archetype is sensorial element, with glazed skin in the portrait.

**Constants.**
- **Palette:** pool turquoise and the jar's aqua, white tile, warm brown skin; lavender only on the kit box.
- **Light:** bright morning sun plus water caustics.
- **Medium:** crisp medium-format digital, neutral-cool, no heavy grain.
- **Skin line:** dewy, unretouched.

**Shots.**
- **Hero:** both women and the jar in use. The professional applies it, a nod to the salon brand.
- **Float:** an overhead scale shot, built as plate plus insert.
- **Surface:** a skin-result portrait, with a Soul style frame.
- **Poolside:** product in world, all three packs large, with the texture beside them.

**Casting.** A Jaipur woman in her late forties and a Kochi woman in her early twenties with deep brown skin, celebrating mature and deep skin in a hydration, not lightening, story.

**The first hero render taught five fixes**, now built into the prompt below:
1. One jar and one lid only (the generator invented a second jar and spare lids).
2. Cream on the cheekbone, well below the eye (it had applied the cream at the lash line).
3. Capsules light aqua, not cobalt.
4. No other people in the pool.
5. The robe the render chose reads spa, so it was adopted across all shots.

## Output (excerpt: header, hero with motion, product shot)

# Pool Blue

**Concept:** Hydration made literal: the jar's own aqua becomes the whole world. An old Bombay public pool on a bright winter morning, crystal-clear turquoise water and white tile, two women and the capsule cream at the water's edge, skin wet, dewy and close enough to see.

**Styling:** The clothes stay simple so the skin leads, white and ivory against turquoise.
- **Jaipur model:** ivory waffle-cotton robe, sleeves pushed up (it reads spa), sitting on the edge with her feet in the water. Hers is the professional hand applying the cream.
- **Kochi model:** plain white square-neck swimsuit, in the water, shoulders and collarbones bare and wet.
- **Products:** HBC Hydrating Boba Cream (₹950), Hydracloud Infusion 9-Step Facial Kit (₹1,696), Face Scrub (₹550). The palette is taken from their packaging.

**Visual system:** Palette: clear pool turquoise and the jar's aqua, white tile, warm brown skin; lavender only on the facial-kit box · Light: bright clear morning sun from high camera left, rippling caustic light across skin and tile, soft fill off the white tile · Medium: Hasselblad X2D 100C, clean crisp medium-format digital, neutral-cool grade, no heavy grain · Hair & makeup: Jaipur model's silver-streaked hair in a low knot, small gold nose ring, bare skin; Kochi model's hair slicked back wet, small gold studs, bare dewy skin; both unretouched

*Assumed:* a bright December morning; crystal-clear water throughout, never murky; a hydration story, never a lightening or "fairness" one; Soul style frames only for the skin-close portrait and the empty overhead plate.

## Why it works

- **Sells:** in every frame you can see that the product hydrates: wet skin, a glistening gel, water everywhere.
- **Packaging owns the world:** the set is the jar's colour.
- **The label is safe:** the final render references the pack only for shape and colour, and the lock pass copies the label.
- **Scale is locked:** every packaging prompt states the jar's true size against her palm and face, so the jar can't grow or shrink between frames.
- **Motion comes from the locked still:** one push-in, fixed caustics, three beats.
- **Variables change, constants hold:** distance (waist-up, overhead wide, face close, macro), people (2, 2 tiny, 1, hands only) and product presence (in hand, none, a melted capsule, three packs).
