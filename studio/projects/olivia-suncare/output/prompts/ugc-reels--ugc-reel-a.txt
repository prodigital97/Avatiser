# REALISM CORE — injected into every Avatiser prompt

Avatiser's entire brand is that our output cannot be clocked as AI. This block
is appended to every compiled prompt AND used as the QC scorecard. A render
that trips even one HARD FAIL below gets regenerated — we do not "fix it in
grading," we re-roll until the tell is gone.

---

## 1. Camera physics — every frame must claim a real camera

Never describe a look abstractly ("cinematic", "high quality", "8k"). Specify
the rig, and the model will inherit its physics:

- **Cinema / hero product:** "shot on ARRI Alexa 35, 50mm Master Prime at
  T2.8, subtle halation in highlights, gentle sensor grain"
- **Lifestyle stills:** "shot on Sony A7 IV, 35mm f/1.8, natural depth of
  field, slight vignetting"
- **Macro product detail:** "100mm macro at f/5.6, shallow focus plane,
  visible micro-texture"
- **UGC / creator:** "filmed on iPhone 15 Pro front camera, 24mm equivalent,
  auto-exposure that breathes, slight rolling-shutter wobble, handheld"

One focal length per shot. Mixed-focal looks inside a single frame is an AI
tell.

## 2. Light must be motivated

Every light source in frame needs a reason to exist. Choose ONE lighting story
per scene and name its source: window left, golden-hour sun low behind
subject, single softbox overhead, practical lamps in background. Forbidden:
"studio lighting" with no direction, rim light appearing from nowhere,
shadows pointing two directions.

## 3. Deliberate imperfection (the anti-render pass)

Perfection is the loudest AI tell. Every prompt includes 2–3 of these,
matched to the medium:

- skin: visible pores, faint asymmetry, flyaway hairs, natural oil sheen on
  T-zone — never "flawless skin"
- surfaces: fingerprints near where a product is held, micro-scratches on
  metal, slight dust in light beams
- environment: cables, worn edges, an object slightly out of place
- optics: subtle chromatic aberration at frame edges, breathing focus in
  video, grain matched to ISO story
- UGC specifically: clipped highlights out a window, imperfect framing,
  a fingertip briefly shading the lens, compression artifacts

## 4. Product label fidelity — HARD RULE

The product's label, logo and packaging text are NEVER text-prompted. They are
always supplied as an image reference (image-to-image / reference
conditioning) from `refs/product/`. If a model cannot take a product
reference, it does not render the product — it renders the scene, and the
product is composited from a reference-conditioned pass. Hallucinated label
text is the #1 instant campaign-killer on Amazon and retail.

## 5. Faces — one identity, locked

Characters are generated once as a Soul 2.0 identity (character-sheet.json
stores the seed/identity id + reference set). Every subsequent appearance uses
that identity reference. Never re-describe a face in words and hope — words
drift, references don't.

## 6. Motion rules (video)

- Real-world physics only: fabric settles, liquid overshoots then rests, hair
  has mass. If a motion could not be filmed with a gimbal, slider, or hand —
  don't prompt it.
- Camera moves: name the rig. "slow 20cm dolly-in", "handheld with natural
  sway", "tripod locked-off". Forbidden: impossible orbit-through-object
  moves, zero-inertia direction changes, speed-ramps inside a 5s clip.
- 24fps for cinema, 30fps for UGC/social. Motion blur consistent with shutter
  at 1/48 or 1/60 respectively.

## 7. The negative block (append to every generation)

```
avoid: waxy or plastic skin, over-smooth surfaces, HDR glow, oversaturated
teal-orange grade, symmetrical face perfection, floating shadows, double
irises, malformed hands, extra fingers, warped or invented label text,
gibberish typography, impossible reflections, light without source, oily
sheen on everything, beauty-filter look, AI art style, illustration,
3D render look, video-game lighting
```

## 8. QC scorecard (run per render before delivery)

HARD FAILS — regenerate immediately:
- [ ] any label/logo/text differs from the reference product
- [ ] hands: wrong finger count, impossible grip
- [ ] face drift from the locked identity
- [ ] shadow/reflection physically impossible
- [ ] motion that violates physics (video)

SOFT FAILS — regenerate if 2+ present:
- [ ] skin or surface reads "airbrushed"
- [ ] lighting direction unclear or contradictory
- [ ] depth of field inconsistent with stated lens
- [ ] grade drifts from campaign palette (scene-sheet)
- [ ] frame too clean — no trace of the imperfection pass

Ship threshold: zero hard fails, ≤1 soft fail, and the "squint test" — at
arm's length, would a media buyer flag this? If yes, re-roll.
