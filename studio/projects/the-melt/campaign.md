# The Melt

**Concept:** The product's own signature moment, a blue capsule melting into skin, shot as a prestige studio beauty campaign. A sky-blue world taken straight from the jar, one beautifully made-up face, and the capsule dissolving under a fingertip into a clear, dewy sheen: hydration you can watch happen.

**Styling:** The beauty comes from casting, professional make-up and light, never from retouching.
- **Kochi model (the campaign's one face):** bare shoulders, a white strapless wrap just in frame; a luminous skin-first base with pores still visible, glowing cheekbones, brushed-up brows, fine lashes, a sheer nude-rose lip, sleek low bun, small gold studs.
- **Product:** HBC Hydrating Boba Cream (₹950), with its clear spatula. Its vivid sky blue owns the set.

**Visual system:** Palette: the jar's vivid sky blue, soft white, warm deep brown skin; nothing else saturated · Light: beauty-dish key from high front-left, white bounce fill below, black flags sculpting the cheekbones, a strip-light rim from behind camera right; neutral colour so skin stays true · Medium: Phase One IQ4 150MP, crisp medium-format digital, clean neutral grade, no grain · Hair & makeup: professional, skin-first and luminous, pores visible, nude-rose lip, sleek low bun, gold studs

*Assumed:* a seamless studio set rather than a location; application on cleansed, dry skin, face and neck, as the product directs; the capsule-melting massage as the hero action; a hydration story, never a lightening or brightening one.
