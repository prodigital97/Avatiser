# Holding Water

**Concept:** Chand Baori was cut thirteen storeys into the dry Rajasthan earth to hold water through the driest months. GYK's hydration line does the same job for skin in an Indian winter: two women at the stepwell's waterline, the blue boba jar the only cool colour in a world of sun-dry sandstone.

**Styling:** The clothes stay out of the way. White and ivory cotton separates cleanly from the ochre stone and leaves the skin, the real subject, bare at the face, shoulders and hands.
- **Jaipur model:** ivory handloom sari, blouse sleeves pushed up like a practitioner at work. Hers is the professional hand applying the cream.
- **Kochi model:** white wrap top, bare shoulders, feet in the water.
- **Products:** HBC Hydrating Boba Cream (₹950), Hydracloud Infusion 9-Step Facial Kit (₹1,696), Face Scrub (₹550). Aqua blue appears only on them.

**Visual system:** Palette: sandstone ochre, shadowed umber, still green water, white cotton; aqua blue only on the products · Light: low winter-morning sun from camera left down the upper steps, lower flights in cool shadow, soft bounce off the water · Medium: Hasselblad 500C/M, Kodak Portra 160 film, fine grain, gentle highlight roll-off · Hair & makeup: Jaipur model's silver-streaked hair in a low knot, small gold nose ring, bare skin; Kochi model's hair tucked behind her ears, small gold studs, bare skin; both skin-first and unretouched

*Assumed:* Chand Baori at Abhaneri as the stepwell; about 8am in December; a hydration story, never a lightening or "fairness" one; casting chosen to celebrate deep and mature skin.
