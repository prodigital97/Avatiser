# Walking Pace

**Concept:** The Darjeeling toy train climbs to Ghum at walking pace, so the restless walk. Two women out-stride the little blue steam train along its own track, dressed in its blue: Nicobar's "winter of wander" made literal.

**Styling:** The clothes *rhyme* with the train and carry the motion.
- **Darjeeling-born model (petite):** Swing Sweater NBI053577 over a long white shirt, Spark Skirt NBI053583, Poppy Hour Scarf NBI054589. Periwinkle flowers echo the carriages; the scarf is the motion flag.
- **Chennai-born Tamil model (tall):** Fir Sweater NBI053543, Kindle Pants NBI055015, Yin-Yang Beanie NBI054549. Berry separates hardest from the blue train; the flare swings with each step.
- Chartreuse lives only on the scarf and the beanie: one accent, used twice.

**Visual system:** Palette: train blue and periwinkle, berry, chocolate brown, mist white; chartreuse only on the scarf and beanie · Light: low winter-morning sun breaking through lifting mist from camera left, soft and directional, raking across the knit · Medium: Leica M6, Kodak Portra 400 film, fine grain, soft halation in the highlights · Hair & makeup: Darjeeling model's long straight black hair loose and wind-moved, wind-flushed bare skin, berry-stained lip; Chennai model's jaw-length loose wavy curls under the beanie, small gold nose stud, bare skin, clear balm lip

*Assumed:* about 7am in late November; the train in its blue livery; a glass of tea as the one prop; faces locked from two approved casting headshots (refs/models/CASTING.md).
